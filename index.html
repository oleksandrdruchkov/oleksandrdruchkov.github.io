1

Збираємо

Беремо вакансії з відкритих джерел: пошукових систем вакансій, дошок оголошень і сайтів роботодавців.

About (English)

UA Jobs Ireland is a non-commercial project that helps Ukrainians living in Ireland, including people under EU temporary protection, find work. We send a weekly email digest of entry-level and mid-level vacancies (warehouse, catering, admin, driving, cleaning, hairdressing and beauty, care, IT support) across Dublin and the rest of Ireland. Each vacancy is marked with the level of English it requires.

Vacancies are collected and pre-screened by our open-source assistant Job Hunter AI. Every listing links back to its original source, and users apply directly with the employer or job board. An on-site job search for Ireland, translated into Ukrainian, is in development.
